Anchored window viewer

Rows are items, columns are days, each entry is the player who receives that item that day. Every c-balance condition says one thing: the anchored window of t columns and r_j = floor((j+c−1)·n/t)+1 rows is j-full — every player appears in it at least j times. Load a CSV, pick t, and the nested windows are drawn darkest-first from j = 1. The checks on the right are recomputed from the loaded file, not read from it.

Load your own latin square in a CSV file

Any square CSV of integers works: rows are items, columns are days, each entry is the player who receives that item that day. Optional # n=, # c= and # mode= comment lines at the top set the balance parameters; otherwise c is taken from the filename, or defaults to 1. You can change c afterwards with the selector above.
